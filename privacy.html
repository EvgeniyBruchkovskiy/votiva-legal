Privacy Policy

Votiva: Rosary & Daily Prayer · Effective date: October 13, 2026

In short: Votiva has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. Your prayer intentions, history and settings stay on your device. We do not collect personal data.

This policy explains how the iOS app “Votiva: Rosary & Daily Prayer” (the “App”), developed by Yevhenii Bruchkovskyi (“we”, “us”), handles information.

1. Information we do not collect

  • No account, registration or sign-in is required or offered.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services, and the App contains no third-party SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites and do not sell or share any data.

2. Data stored only on your device

Your prayer intentions, prayer history and settings are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Version 1.0 does not sync this data through iCloud. If you delete the App, this data is deleted from your device.

3. Content downloads

The App downloads prayer audio and a content catalog from our static content delivery network, hosted on Firebase Hosting (Google). As with any web server, the hosting provider may process standard server logs — such as IP address, time of request and the requested file — to deliver the content and keep the service secure. We do not use these logs to identify users. These requests contain no information about your prayers, intentions or settings.

4. Purchases

Votiva Plus subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. The App only receives confirmation from Apple of whether you have an active purchase. Apple’s handling of your data is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

5. Notifications

The App uses local notifications only — a daily prayer reminder, novena reminders and a reminder before a free trial ends. They are scheduled on your device and no data is sent to any server. You can turn them off at any time in the App or in iOS Settings.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

7. Users in the EU/EEA and the UK (GDPR)

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not carry out profiling or automated decision-making about you. Data stored on your device is under your control. The only processing related to the App is the delivery of content by our hosting provider described in section 3, based on our legitimate interest in delivering and securing the App’s content. If you have any questions about your data or your rights, please contact us at thewayprod1@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above.

9. Contact
